Our Gastronomic Heritage in the Capital

Located along Kabanbay Batyr Avenue in Astana, Tary Ethno Cafe revives ancient Kazakh nomadic traditions through modern artisanal brewing. The soul of our kitchen is tary—millet grains prepared using traditional ancestral methods. We believe specialty coffee culture flourishes when enriched by authentic regional memory.

Our mission is honoring the deep warmth of Kazakh hospitality by combining roasted millet with modern specialty espresso, remarked head barista Aigerim Kassymova on September 10, 2026.

Guests can explore distinct beverage profiles, such as our signature espresso served at exactly 92°C, accompanied by traditional dairy confections. As noted in our archival records, millet cultivation sustained nomadic clans across the Great Steppe for centuries.

Weekly Cultural Highlights

Join us every Thursday evening for live dombra performances and ethno-fusion acoustic sessions. Visitors frequently enjoy our seasonal desserts with organic honey sourced from Eastern Kazakhstan.

Visiting Hours & Location

We are situated at Kabanbay Batyr Avenue 15/1 in Astana, KZ. We welcome guests daily from 08:00 until 23:00.
