Manual Pour-Over Extraction Process

  1. Grinding 18g of fresh medium-roast beans.
  2. Rinsing paper filter with clean hot water.
  3. Blooming grounds for 40 seconds.
  4. Pouring filtered water until 300ml.

Our extraction water maintains optimal mineral balance with pure H2O and magnesium.
