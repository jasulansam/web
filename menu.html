A guide to pour-over coffee

  1. Grinding 18g of fresh medium-roast beans.
  2. Rinsing paper filter with clean hot water.
  3. Blooming grounds for 40 seconds.
  4. Pouring filtered water until 300ml.

This is a general brewing guide. Pour-over availability is not confirmed by the published menu. Water (H 2 O), grind and pouring speed all affect extraction.
