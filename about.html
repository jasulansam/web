Laptop-Friendly Environment

We believe good coffee fuels creativity. Our space features an accessible entrance ramp for guests with disabilities, soft instrumental background music, and dedicated quiet zones. We take pride in being a welcoming place where guests can write code, finish university research papers, or enjoy quiet personal reading without rushing.

Carefully Sourced Coffee Beans

Our house blend combines washed Arabica beans from Guji, Ethiopia, and Huila, Colombia. We roast in small batches to highlight bright floral notes, peach sweetness, and a creamy chocolate finish.
